Which wing today

Mode

16 reps, about five minutes. Spanish positional play (juego de posición) cuts the pitch into five lanes: two wide lanes, two inside lanes (pasillos interiores; English coaches call them half-spaces) and the middle. Spread out: no more than two team-mates in a lane and no more than three on a line, so whoever has the ball always has two or three options. A few seconds of play, then it freezes as the last pass is struck: tap where you should be.

We have it — you are the width (amplitud), level with their last defender: on the touchline when the ball is on your side, pinning their full-back (fijar al lateral); a few metres in when it is central; at the inside edge of your lane when it is far. If your full-back takes your lane on your line, you take the half-space.
They have it — you are one of the midfield four (línea de medios): level with your 8s, closing your half-space, sliding with the block (basculación); jump to their full-back as the ball travels to him (saltar al lateral).
We just lost it — press straight away (presión tras pérdida): their outlet right by you, you press him; away from you, do not chase the ball: take the man nearest you who could get the next pass — usually their full-back — and shut the pass to him.

Learn paints the lanes on the grass and shows everyone on the map when you tap. Test takes all of that away.

Scan: while it plays, tap the left or right half of the screen (on a computer, the ← → keys) to look over that shoulder — a quick 90° turn of the head and straight back to the ball, about a third of a second, like a pro.
